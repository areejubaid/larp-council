It costs cents per session, so we can ship a pilot by Friday. |

## If a judge pitches their own idea

The offline scorer rewards:
- **Length:** more than about 40 words (a few sentences).
- **Specifics:** words like "first hundred users", "customers", "cost", "ship", "Friday", "pilot".
- **Penalties:** clichés like "Uber for", "on the blockchain", "but with AI", and impossible claims like "quantum" or "AGI".

A short or vague pitch lands on sarcastic or appalled. A detailed pitch with users, cost and a timeline gets respect or the slow clap. With an API key the scoring is real and none of this applies.

## Demo order (2 minutes)

1. Bad idea first for the rage quit.
2. The slow clap pitch next.
3. LARP round with a judge: pick the Perfectionist, then pitch in their style (words like "simple", "focus", "feel", "experience" raise the Character Consistency Score).

Wake the server first: open https://larp-council-brain.onrender.com/health a few minutes before.
